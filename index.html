Research & projects
  • Degenerative repetition in language models. Completed, Sep. 2026.
    Research internship at ENSIAS, supervised by Prof. Boutaina Hdioud.
    A triggered repetition penalty: a multi-scale detector gates the standard penalty so it only fires when the model actually starts looping. It matches the industry-standard penalty on both repetition and fluency while leaving the logits untouched 40% of the time. The second half of the work showed that anti-repetition decoding does not remove repetition but displaces it from the lexical to the semantic level — a failure mode standard n-gram metrics cannot see — and established why a hidden-state detector for it is valid in aggregate but not as a per-case decision signal. Technical report
  • Chest X-ray report generation with margin-based DPO. Ongoing.
    A missed urgent finding costs far more than a clumsy sentence, so the preference objective should not treat both the same. A Direct Preference Optimization variant with an asymmetric margin penalizes critical diagnostic errors more heavily than benign ones. Qwen2-VL fine-tuned with SFT and DPO under a 6 GB VRAM budget, preference data built from MIMIC-CXR, clinical evaluation via CheXbert. Doubled the F1 score on urgent cases. Technical report
  • Chess coaching platform. Ongoing.
    Team project. Most chess tools train tactics; this one targets strategy, which nothing covers well. I built the analytics engine: rating prediction from raw game histories (stacked XGBoost, Ridge, Random Forest), a CNN trained on 588k positions to detect 20 tactical patterns, and playing-style embeddings learned by inverse reinforcement learning over 24 strategic patterns.
  • Multi-agent octopus simulation. Personal project.
    Three independently-controlled appendages, each with its own reactive policy, coordinated by a quantized 1-bit LLM acting as a high-level planner. Deadlocks between agents were resolved through an emergent peer-to-peer channel; locomotion trained with a GPU-batched genetic algorithm.
